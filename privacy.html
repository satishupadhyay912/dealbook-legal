Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

Dealbook ("the app") is made by the Dealbook team in India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept and the choices you have. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, and Google Play's requirements.

The short version

  • Your deals, brands, contacts, invoices and payments are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The app has no account and no cloud storage of its own.
  • We use Google Firebase to collect app usage statistics and crash reports, only after you agree to this policy by tapping Get started. These do not include your deal details or the people you work with.
  • Backups, invoices and messages go only where you choose to send them.

Information stored on your phone

When you use the app you may enter:

  • Your profile: name, Instagram handle, follower count, niches, email address, phone number, billing address, state, UPI ID, PAN, GSTIN and SAC code.
  • Brands and their contacts: brand names, contact names, roles, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses and GSTINs.
  • Deals: fees, deliverables, deadlines, notes, payments, TDS amounts, invoices and payment reminders you send.

This information is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see it, and it is not uploaded anywhere by the app. It is used only to show your deals, create invoices, calculate amounts and send you reminders.

Some of this information is about other people (for example, a brand manager's phone number). Please only add details that you are allowed to use for your work with them.

Information collected automatically

After you agree to this policy (by tapping Get started on the first screen, or restoring a backup there), the app uses two Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics records how the app is used, for example that an invoice was created or a payment was recorded, plus whether it was a tax invoice or a barter deal. It also collects standard device information such as phone model, Android version, app version, language, approximate location (country or city, from your IP address) and an app instance identifier.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed, device model, Android version and an installation identifier.

These events never include your names, amounts, brand details, contact details, PAN, GSTIN, UPI ID or invoice contents.

We use this information only to understand which features are used and to fix bugs. Google processes it on our behalf under its own terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and keeps it for a limited period. We do not sell this information or combine it with other data to identify you. The app does not show ads today; if that changes, we will update this policy and tell you in the app.

When information leaves your phone

Information leaves your phone only when you choose to send it:

  • Sharing an invoice or a CSV export opens Android's share menu. The file goes to the app or person you pick.
  • Sending a payment reminder opens WhatsApp or your email app with the message filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it there.
  • Backups are saved to the location you pick, such as Google Drive or your phone's storage. Anyone with access to that file can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Help & feedback opens your email app to write to us.

Once information is in another app or service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Android may also include the app's data in your device backup (Google One / Android backup) if you have turned that on in your phone settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you about content deadlines and overdue payments. You can turn this off at any time in Android settings.
  • Internet: used only by Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.

The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location services or SMS.

How long information is kept

Information on your phone stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes it permanently unless you made a backup. Analytics and crash data is kept by Google for a limited period and then deleted automatically.

Your rights and choices

Under Indian law you have the right to know what personal data is processed, to correct or delete it, to withdraw consent, to nominate someone to act for you and to raise a complaint.

  • You can view, edit and delete all data you entered directly in the app.
  • To stop analytics and crash reporting, uninstall the app or contact us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing done before.
  • To ask about or delete analytics data linked to your app installation, email us.

Children

Dealbook is meant for adults (18 and over) who work with brands. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox and your phone's lock screen. Keep your phone and any backup files secure. No method of storage is completely secure, but we keep the information we receive to a minimum.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and the new version will be available in the app under Profile. For important changes we will also tell you in the app or in the Play Store release notes. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
